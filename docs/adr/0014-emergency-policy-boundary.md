# ADR-0014: Emergency policy is separate and fail-safe

Status: Accepted

## DECISION

Emergency handling is not ordinary conversational reasoning.

If the system reaches `EMERGENCY_ESCALATION`, backend policy forces handoff/escalation regardless of the LLM's proposed next action.

Detection rules themselves must come from a clinic-approved, versioned emergency policy provider. This repository does not invent symptom thresholds or medical triage rules.

## WHY

The assistant is not a doctor and cannot improvise diagnosis, urgency classification or treatment instructions.

Emergency policy must be auditable, versioned and approved independently of model prompt changes.

## INVARIANTS

- emergency intent cannot continue into normal FAQ/booking response;
- emergency escalation does not depend on containment KPI;
- triggered policy assessment requires rule_id + ruleset_version + reason;
- no diagnosis/prescription/treatment guarantee is generated by this boundary;
- the exact escalation destination and public wording remain clinic-policy configuration.
